## The planner view

One page, no mathematics. It says what to do and what it is worth:

* 472 orders were reviewed, 66 are recommended for reassignment
* 40,771 more cases delivered, fill rate 90.5% to 93.3%
* \$52,416 of customer penalties avoided, \$57,401 of extra freight paid
* net gain \$1,929,835

It also lists the largest recommendations one by one, shows which DCs receive the volume, and gives
three things to check before releasing the plan.
